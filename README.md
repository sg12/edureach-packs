# EduReach question packs

Downloadable content packs for the EduReach (Five Credits) app. The app fetches them at runtime; they are not bundled into the build.

- Ghana (WASSCE): https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sg12/edureach-packs/main/ghana/manifest.json

Questions are original, written to the published syllabus format; they are not official WAEC material. Errors: use "Report question" in the app.

Do not edit by hand: packs are generated by scripts/build_ghana_pack.py in the app repository.
